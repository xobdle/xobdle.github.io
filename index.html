About Xobdle

Xobdle is a free daily Assamese word-guessing game. A new five-akshara Assamese word is served every day, and players get five attempts to find it.

Xobdle was created to make playing with Assamese words simple, quick and fun. No account is required to play.

A new Xobdle is available every day at 6:00 AM IST.

Inspired by Wordle

Xobdle is an independent game inspired by Wordle and adapted for Assamese words and aksharas.

Xobdle is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wordle or The New York Times.

Privacy

Xobdle does not require an account to play and does not ask players to provide a name, phone number or other personal information as part of the game.

Game data

Xobdle may store game-related information in your browser so that features such as your current game, completed game and daily-play status can work correctly.

Website analytics

Xobdle uses GoatCounter to understand general website usage, such as visits to the site. This information helps understand how Xobdle is being used and helps improve the game.

Email updates

Xobdle does not require an email address to play. If you voluntarily choose to join Xobdle email updates, you will be directed to a Google Form where you may provide your email address.

Third-party services

Xobdle may rely on third-party services for functions such as website analytics, website hosting and optional email sign-up. Those services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Sale of information

Xobdle does not sell personal information.

Changes to this privacy notice

This privacy notice may be updated when Xobdle introduces new features or services.
